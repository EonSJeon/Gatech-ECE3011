# SolidWorks Arm Assembly Recordings

Three screen recordings document the arm assembly and mechanism development in SolidWorks.
The dates are taken from the original recording filenames.

| Recording | Duration | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Arm mating and interference check](Arm_Mating_and_Interference_Check_2023-10-14.mp4) | 1:40 | Positioning arm components, applying assembly mates, and checking interference. |
| [Arm, servo, and bow mechanism assembly](Arm_Servo_and_Bow_Mechanism_Assembly_2023-10-14.mp4) | 43:10 | Assembling servo mounts and the bow mechanism, editing parts, and refining assembly mates. |
| [Arm and bow movement demonstration](Arm_and_Bow_Movement_Demo_2023-10-15.mp4) | 0:58 | Manually moving and rotating the assembled arm and bow mechanism in SolidWorks. |

These are CAD development recordings, not physical hardware demonstrations or validated motion studies.
The files contain different stages of the work; all three are retained without re-encoding.
